## plants (2807)

- (2815) report: implemented the isolated daily mechanism and kingdom-neutral tissue populations; not integrated or field validated. Used forms: `plants.forms.canopy_light_interception` (Beer-Lambert), `plants.forms.canopy_extinction_k` (general K only when the species has no K row; a present null row throws), `plants.forms.water_light_at_depth` (constant-Kd attenuation), `plants.forms.rue_definition` (absorbed PAR to dry mass), `plants.forms.temperature_beta_function` (only its sourced zero-base case), `plants.forms.fao56_ks` (remaining available water / ((1-p) TAW)), `plants.forms.salinity_threshold_slope`, `plants.forms.critical_nutrient_concentration` (critical-concentration definition; the linear tissue fraction is Rex's rule), `plants.forms.liebig_law_of_minimum`, `plants.forms.stress_combination_multiplicative`, `plants.forms.respiration_growth_maintenance`, and `plants.forms.respiration_q10` (species Q10, 25 C reference). Equation rows' null numeric Value is intentional: a researched equation in Note is a definition, not a missing numeric constant. An absent/unsourced definition or null required numeric constant raises PlantFormError. No library rows were added or filled.
- (2815) unavailable forms: `plants.forms.ph_trapezoid`, `plants.forms.temperature_cardinal_beta_function` (nonzero Tmin), `plants.forms.salinity_beta_function`, `plants.forms.photosynthetic_oxygen_per_carbon`, `plants.forms.growth_respiration_fraction_of_assimilate`, `plants.forms.inundation_response`, and `plants.forms.allometry_height_diameter` are absent (unavailable, equivalent to null), and their paths throw named errors. The growth-respiration schema says fraction of total respiration, whereas DESIGN 1.3 requests a fraction of positive post-maintenance assimilate: that convention needs a sourced resolution before production use. `plants.forms.allometry_agb_eq4` exists but cannot yield both height and diameter from biomass without density and a second size relation; the schema supplies neither and 2808 flags its output unit. Tree creation therefore throws after reporting missing species values. No guessed allometry, oxygen ratio, shifted temperature curve, pH curve or flooding response is used with production data. Test-only in-memory forms explicitly supply the missing equations/constants to exercise the engine; they are not scientific validation or production defaults. Because the pH form is unavailable, even a complete synthetic species using the production forms stops with PlantFormError at that path.
- (2815) assumptions and units: allocation across an organ's populations is proportional to viable mass (level 4); a single empty population receives its organ's allocation, but multiple empty populations have undefined shares and throw. A meristem population exists only when the caller's species table supplies a meristem_fraction; the current schema has none. New populations start intact, uninjured and nonfibrotic as initial-state definitions. Organ capacity is mean capacity times one minus mean fibrotic fraction, both weighted by viable mass; matrix integrity is independently weighted and has no capacity coupling until DUP-67. Tissue injury, repair and matrix-change rates are null: no plant tissue-response source has been opened. Anatomy and human modules are untouched.
- (2815) limitations: CO2 response is explicitly the unsourced neutral factor 1; VPD and CO2 are exposed inputs but unused, with no ambient value substituted. Water-use efficiency gives gross-assimilation water demand, capped by supply; water status is the remaining available-water/TAW fraction, not a sourced relative-water-content response. This standalone API accepts PAR in MJ/m2/day, ground area in m2, and per-individual root-zone water/TAW and nutrient supplies in kg. DESIGN's photon-mol PAR and volumetric soil-water ports need explicit upstream conversions; no spectral energy, field-capacity or wilting-point value is assumed. A 365-day model year and percent/gram conversions are unit conventions. Reserve is dry carbohydrate mass accounted with the supplied carbon_fraction; reserve-specific chemistry is not available. Tissue N/P/K is a uniform whole-organism concentration; oxidation releases its nutrients as litter residue, and terminal death exports remaining tissue as litter. Initial water and nutrient states are caller inputs. Maturity uses age_at_maturity and nonzero living biomass (and lifespan when supplied); a quantitative size-at-maturity threshold is absent from the schema and not invented. No convergence test or host wiring is claimed.
- (2815) unvalidated: no complete executable species profile is available (the task began with none filled; subsequent research still leaves required values null). Field validation belongs to the per-species demonstration units after missing forms and profiles are sourced. The synthetic species and supplementary forms in test/plant.test.ts are fixtures only. Step convergence belongs to LIFE-11 when connected to the host. Status: implemented in isolation, not integrated, not validated.
