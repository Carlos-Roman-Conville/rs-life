(1) Conditions the sources disagree on: seed mass (Francis about 7,000 seeds/kg and the Tropical Tree Seed Manual 5,400 to 7,800 against the Agroforestree Database 3,350 to 3,500); age at first seed (Francis 12 to 13 years, Tropical Tree Seed Manual 10 to 25, Agroforestree 20 and usually 30 to 40); dispersal distance (PROTA up to 500 m, Tropical Tree Seed Manual usually within 1 or 2 tree lengths); drought (UF/IFAS 'high' tolerance, Agroforestree 'a complete failure in dry localities'); soil (Francis marl and porous limestone, pH about 5.0 to 8.5, PROTA 'often on limestone', Agroforestree 'deep, rich soil' and 'avoids stiff, heavy soils'); pH 5.0 is confounded with rainfall above 2250 mm in the Puerto Rico plantation failures. (2) Pollination and propagation against DESIGN.md 1.5: insect pollination and winged seed dispersed by wind are both confirmed by opened pages (Francis, PROTA, Agroforestree, Tropical Tree Seed Manual), but the pollinators differ by source: bees and moths (Francis, from Styles and Khosla 1976), bees, moths and thrips (PROTA), and thrips alone in a Florida study (Howard, Nakahara and Williams 1995) that was not opened (403), so the pollinator unit should open it first. PROTA says self-pollination may occur, so self_compatible stays null until a pollination test is found. Flowering is reported as shortly before the rainy season, a timing and not a tested cue, so DESIGN.md 1.5's rule that phenology follows a sourced cue is not met for this species. Fruit set is low (usually one fruit per inflorescence, about 10 male flowers to each female), which the pollinator unit needs. (3) The schema has no salinity or drought rows for a tree that is not a mangrove, so the tolerances found are only in notes here: UF/IFAS rates aerosol salt tolerance high and drought tolerance high, Francis says it colonises areas that receive salt spray, and an Egyptian S. mahagoni seedling study reported in a result snippet (all alive at 4.69 dS/m, survival about 64 to 70 percent at 6.25 dS/m) was not opened. (4) Validation study: Briscoe 1962 (Guanica, Puerto Rico), kept out of the profile. The profile's max_stem_diameter draws on Weaver and Francis 1988 instead, a different study; PROTA gives 0.55 cm/yr for Cuba and Weaver and Francis give 0.86 cm/yr at Estate Thomas, St. Croix, neither used for a row.
