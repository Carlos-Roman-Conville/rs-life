Cultivars: Creole cane is a Java-ribbon-type hybrid, Otaheite replaced it c. 1790 to 1792 and gave about 1/3 more juice, but no opened source states a ratooning difference. Pollination and propagation match DESIGN.md 1.5: the plantation crop is propagated vegetatively from stem cuttings and by ratooning (clonal_propagation yes), natural pollination is by wind with mostly self-sterile flowers (TNAU), and flowering matters only for breeding, so the crop needs no pollinator population.
