- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere grid transport rates. `gasdynamics.grid` gives the rules (advection by the wind, diffusion by "D x step / cell size squared", buoyancy, deposition) as `deferred` text with no D, no rise or settle rate and no wind field. The host supplies `GridRates.diffusion`, `rise` and `settle` (shares per step); each is `null` (off) unless given, and the wind is the host's `windMS`. The stability factor of `atmosphere.modifiers` (dispersion x0.3 on a calm night) is not applied: the host scales `diffusion` itself.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere deposition and sinks other than ventilation (`sources_sinks` "surfaces, water, rain": SO2 and H2S lost to wet surfaces, particles settle "half in an hour in still air", rain washes smoke; `grid.deposition`). The rates are "placeholders" with no number; particles and smoke are not registry substances. Not implemented.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere source rates the rows do not give: a lamp's CO "if it gutters", a brazier's O2 and CO2 (only "CO 0.5 L/min in a shut cabin"; charcoal burns by `combustionProcess`), a fire's own O2 (derived from the carbon: O2 = CO2 + CO / 2) and smoke ("1 g/min", no smoke substance), black powder (330 cm3 of gas per gram, but no split among CO2, N2, CO, H2S, H2, CH4), rot's CH4 and CO2, plants ("negligible"), natural vents ("by the place"). - UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere livestock "as a person x body-mass ratio": the body source now uses physiology metabolic power divided by oxygen energy, times the respiratory quotient 0.8 (`gasdynamics.box_model`), which needs no reference mass; it reproduces the person row (0.25 O2, 0.2 CO2 at 70 kg and 1 MET) to 3% and the goat row (0.1 at 28 kg).
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere the A-a gradient's rise with lung damage ("5 to 10 mmHg in health, more with damage", `anatomy.respiratory`), exchange capacity (diffusing capacity, mL O2 per min per mmHg), airway resistance, dead space, tissue integrity and the transfer `capacity x gradient`: no number for how any of them changes the gradient. `RespiratoryProfile.aaGradientMmHg` is the caller's lever; the others are not used. Gills and water breathing (`medium` water or other) give null outputs and no exchange.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere shifts of the saturation curve by pH, temperature and CO2, and the left shift by COHb (`circulation.blood_gas`, "shifted by..."): the library has no coefficients. SaO2 is the plain Severinghaus form. The oxygen content with CO (`CaO2`) is the circulation module's.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere ventilation when the CO2 drive fails ("over 70 mmHg the drive fails and sleepiness wins", `physiology.ventilation`): no number for the fall. The drive is held at its 70 mmHg value (and the profile maximum) above it.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere the uptake fraction of a gas in the inhaled dose (`inhaledDoseLitres`; CHEMISTRY): the library names it without a value, so the caller passes it. Exposure integration is ppm-minutes and the peak per gas; COHb is the only dose model.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere mixing that "restores" the x1.5 low and x0.5 high layers (`gasdynamics.box_model`): no rate, so `layeredPpm` takes `mixed` as the caller's flag; CO (0.97 x air) and the other gases the row does not name read the mean. - NOTE: atmosphere the pirate dashboard's respiratory calculator coefficients that no table carries are used as cited and are illustrative there: arterial PO2 = alveolar PO2 less 7 mmHg (the library says 5 to 10), PaCO2 = 40 + FiCO2 (P - 47) x 0.7 for retained inspired CO2, the hypoxic drive (when PAO2 is under 60 and CO2 under 2%, PaCO2 falls by 0.5 per mmHg short, at most 15, to a floor of 25; ventilation rises by drop / 15), and the COHb equilibrium cap of 0.8. - NOTE: atmosphere the altitude table's resting saturation at 2,256, 3,000 and 5,000 m (0.90, 0.88, 0.75) is a typical reading that includes some acclimatisation (over-breathing), which the formula only models by the calculator's hypoxic drive: it gives 0.876, 0.845 and 0.701 (0.971, 0.961 and 0.947 at 0, 500 and 1,000 m against 0.97, 0.96 and 0.95). - NOTE: atmosphere a flow in litres per minute is litres of gas at the room's own temperature and pressure (`gasdynamics.units`, moles = p V / (R T)); physiology's oxygen consumption is usually quoted at standard conditions, about 9% more moles per litre at 25 C. The sources are placeholders to that precision.
- NOTE: atmosphere the outside air's water, 0.012 kg per kg of dry air (`OUTSIDE_WATER_KG_PER_KG`), is the still's value, moved with the room box from `process.ts` unchanged; the baseline table gives only "0 to 4% by humidity". - NOTE: atmosphere breathing moves O2 from the room to the body and CO2 from the body to the room as traced exchanges (`mass:o2`, `mass:co2`, process `breathing`); nutrition's pools do not yet book the carbon and oxygen of the oxidised food, so a per-element ledger that includes the body would not close. `physiologyProcess` integrates exposure through `accumulateExposure` (ppm-minutes and the peak per gas) and `breathingProcess` does not repeat it.
