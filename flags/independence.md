## independence (2532)

- UNSUPPORTED: horse feed. `nutrition.foods` has no hay, grass or oats row (`oatmeal-dry` is a human food, not horse oats), and the horse's absorption (`anatomy.species.diet-and-absorption`, hindgut) has no numbers, so `feedAssembly` stays disabled for the horse. `examples/horse-stable.ts` feeds kcal and water only (`ingest`), a host ration of 1 x the species' Kleiber RMR; no nutrient pools are simulated.
- UNSUPPORTED: horse work MET, PHYSIOLOGY.md. `physiology.met` carries no horse, riding or draught row. The two scheduled working hours run at rest (workMet null in the ledger); no work energy or heat is claimed.
- UNSUPPORTED: horse respiratory profile, Hb, skin area, sweat rate, water share and body mass. `anatomy.respiratory` is human only, the species row has no horse haemoglobin (circulation/DO2 stays off, so cardiac output is the profile row x mass, reported not simulated), and the thermal/sweat model in physiology is the human's: ISO 7730 per m2, Guyton's urine, and the 1.9 m2 and h 10 that `examples/horse-stable.ts` now declares itself, since physiology no longer defaults them (2540). The horse breathes the human per-kg profile as a named stand-in. 500 kg and the human fixture's water share are host circumstances, not library values.
- UNSUPPORTED: horse muscle layout (`anatomy.species.muscle-layout-muscular-groups`: "a data task"), so muscle fatigue is null; the ledger's fatigue is MET-hours only. Sleep and alertness tables are human and not reported for the horse.
- NOTE: `physiology.restingMetabolicRate` reads the species' RMR formula through the `physiologyProcess` option `rmrFormula` (Kleiber 70 x kg^0.75 for the horse; `anatomy.speciesRmr` delegates to the same function). The ledger's mass is a stored-energy proxy (stored kcal / the library's fat energy density), not wet mass.
- NOTE: the gate greps `src` and `examples` for the game's name case-insensitively; four source comments that named the host game were reworded to "host".
