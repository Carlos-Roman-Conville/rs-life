- NOTE (2540): water out is now sweat, skin diffusion, breath and urine; water in is drink, food water and metabolic water. Breath (respiratory latent heat 1.7e-5 M (5867 - pa)), skin diffusion (3.05e-3 (5733 - 6.99 (M - W) - pa)) and the respiratory dry heat (0.0014 M (34 - ta)) are ISO 7730:2005's PMV terms; their water is the latent heat over `SWEAT_LATENT` (ENG-LAT). Urine is Guyton and Hall ch. 29: 600 mOsm/day at 1,200 mOsm/L at most (0.5 L/day, always passed) and 50 mOsm/L at least (12 L/day), the kidney passing any surplus over the starting water up to that most dilute rate. Metabolic water is stoichiometry: glucose and palmitic acid give one H2O per CO2, so water = RQ x O2, with the library's RQ (gasdynamics box_model, 0.8) and the O2 of the MET cost at physics.ts's 20.1 kJ/L (STPD). In the checkpoint-1 week that is about 0.5 kg/day, against Guyton's 0.2 L/day for a sedentary adult.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology the urine numbers and their timing. 600 mOsm/day is Guyton's 70 kg adult on an ordinary diet; it is not computed from the meal's protein and salt, not scaled by body mass (the horse passes a human's), and the kidney answers a surplus in the same step (no hour-long water diuresis lag, no ADH clock: CIRCULATION's volume loop has no time constant either). Faecal water (Guyton: 0.1 L/day) has no row and is not modelled.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology metabolic water from protein (its nitrogen leaves as urea, a different H2O/O2) and from stored fat as a triglyceride (tripalmitin gives 49 H2O per 51 CO2, 4% less than the fatty-acid form used); the fuel mix is the RQ's, not nutrition's actual oxidation (the body element ledger stays unsupported).
- UNSUPPORTED (2540): a sweat-rate fit by MET and WBGT (the ACSM 2007 position stand's table, ISO 7933's predicted heat strain, Gagge or Havenith fits). WebFetch and WebSearch were denied in session 2540, so no page could be opened. This is recorded once and not retried. No library row holds a sweat rate. Sweat is therefore ISO 7933's *concept* of required evaporation, computed with ISO 7730's terms (Ereq = M - W - Cres - Eres - C - R at the comfort skin temperature 35.7 - 0.028 (M - W), less the skin diffusion), and not ISO 7933's own skin-temperature, wettedness and efficiency equations. WBGT enters through the core drift (`wbgt_limits`), and through the core row's "sweat rate at max" above 38.5 C, whose maximum is the hydration table source's "over 2.5 L/h". Air temperature and humidity (the WBGT inputs) set the heat balance itself.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology the comfort skin temperature outside comfort. ISO 7730's 35.7 - 0.028 (M - W) is the skin temperature a person at that activity finds comfortable (26 to 27 C at MET 6). In heat the real skin is warmer, so this model overstates the heat gained from hot air and the sweat it requires. In cool still air it understates the evaporative maximum, which is 16.5 hc (psat(skin) - pa), nude, with no clothing resistance. The checkpoint-1 week therefore drips about 0.9 kg of its 2.4 kg of daily sweat. Dripped sweat leaves the body (to a `drip` sink) without cooling it, and its sodium is still lost. The uncooled heat stays in the lumped tissue. ISO 7933's sweat = Ereq / r (more sweat when it drips) is not applied. Clothing (ISO 9920 clo) and a mean radiant temperature other than the air's are not inputs.
