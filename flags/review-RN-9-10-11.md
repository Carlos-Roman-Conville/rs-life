It fails for a room under about 0.5 m³ with that wall area, or any thin component with small mass and large `h·area_m2`. No assertion in code.

## RN-10 Gases and chemical reactions

1. **Relationships and domains.**
   - Ideal gas (`R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹`, `physics.ts:7`; moles `pV/RT`, `litresToMoles` atmosphere.ts:67): valid at room and vessel pressures; no real-gas correction (not stated in code). Domain limit: vessel gas at 600 to 1100 °C and near 1 atm is within ideal-gas accuracy; high-pressure sealed vessels are not checked.
   - Room box, `atmosphere.ts:141`: `V dC/dt = −Q (C − C_out)` discretised as `frac = min(1, ACH·dt/3600)` of the difference per step (explicit Euler). The first-order exact step would be `1 − exp(−ACH·dt/3600)`; the two differ by `O((ACH·dt/3600)²)` (at 20 ACH and 1 s: 1.5e-5 relative). Well-mixed assumption; the library box_model row supplies the uniform tolerance (10 %) and the heavier-gas layer factors (×1.5 low, ×0.5 high), which the code labels placeholders from that row.
   - Gas sources and sinks (`lampFlows`, `fireFlows`, `rotFlows`, `sulphurFlows`): rates read from library rows ("Rate (placeholder)"); not opened; they are the library's, so unconfirmed as sourced rates here.
   - Grid, `atmosphere.ts:269`: operator-split explicit advection (upwind), diffusion share, buoyancy and settling shares. The library gives no numbers for diffusion, rise or settle (host supplies them); the code states this. Domain: transport shares are host-chosen fractions per step, not physical coefficients.
   - Reactions, `reactions.ts:101`: stoichiometric records, threshold ignition, first-order Arrhenius `k = k0 exp(−Ea/RT)` consuming `1 − exp(−k dt)` of the limiting reagent at the step's initial temperature, otherwise complete within the step. Arrhenius reference opened? No (IUPAC Gold Book link in the README, not opened this session): **unconfirmed**. Domain of a first-order law with a frozen temperature: valid when T changes little within a step. All four seed records use complete-within-step (`data/reactions.json`, confirmed by reading them).

4. **Numerical method.**
   - Reactions: sequential in record order on the live vessel state, algebraic per step, `extent = min(reagent moles / coefficient)`, then capped by the Arrhenius fraction, the oxygen floor (`oxygenFloorExtent`) and, for endothermic rows, the heat budget above the minimum temperature. Reaction order changes the result when two records draw on the same reagent: `comb_c_full` and `water_formation` both consume O2; the first record takes what it needs and the second sees the remainder. No negative amount can result (each extent is bounded by the current contents), but the split is by record order, not proportional. No test fixes this behaviour.
   - Arrhenius convergence in Δt: with the temperature frozen at the step start, `1 − exp(−k dt)` is the exact first-order solution for constant T, so n steps of dt/n at constant T reproduce one step of dt to rounding. The only Δt error is the temperature change inside a step (exothermic rows heat the vessel within the step; the next step sees the new T). No test composes steps; the existing Arrhenius test checks one step against the closed form at four (T, dt) pairs.
   - Gas transport is instantaneous (named); the first outgoing gas edge only.
   - Room box: explicit, clamp `frac ≤ 1`. Grid: diffusion share must satisfy `6 k ≤ 1` (throws, tested); advection `|u| dt / cell` is clamped to 1 silently (F-7); the settle and rise share is clamped to `min(1, share · |ρ − 1|)`.
   - Element ledger: per-step check by `conserveElements()` (tolerance in `world.ts`, not read).
   - No step-convergence test for any gas or reaction process.

## Findings

- F-1 (RN-9): the evaporation trace records the carried enthalpy as `kg · CP.water · T` (`process.ts:96`, 4186 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹) while the room is given `kg · CP.waterVapour · T` (`process.ts:92`, 1996); the trace and the state disagree by `kg · 2190 · T` (131 kJ per kg at 60 °C against 120 kJ in the room).
- F-2 (RN-9): `wallsProcess` computes `uOut = 1 / (1/U − 1/h)` with no guard (`process.ts:109`); a wall with `U ≥ h` (e.g. 10 or more) gives an infinite or negative `uOut` and a non-physical outside flow.
- F-3 (RN-9): no stability or overshoot guard for the explicit exchange in `lossesProcess` (`process.ts:62-73`) and `wallsProcess` (`process.ts:104-115`); `dt · hA / C > 1` overshoots the equilibrium and no test or throw states the limit.
- F-4 (RN-9): Churchill–Chu (cylinder in quiescent fluid, F-Chart page) is used for the condenser's coolant stream at 1 kg/min with properties fixed at 40 °C (`physics.ts:122`, `process.ts:163`); the geometry (cylinder inside the tub or tube carrying a stream) and the property error at film temperatures above 40 °C are not stated in the code.
