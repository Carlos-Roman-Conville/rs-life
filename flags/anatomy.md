## anatomy (2527)

- NOTE: `src/anatomy.ts` builds registry organ components and typed blood supply/return edges, tissue components (including muscle), and explicit graph adequacy inputs. Organ ids are the library's bare ids (`heart`, `kidneys`, etc.); tissues retain their full library ids. Shell mass is zero because these are views into the host body's already-counted mass; unknown organ masses are `null`, not zero-size organs. Tissue structural wiring follows the `Where` column. Blood volume/Hb transfers remain in circulation, gas transfers in atmosphere, and food/water/nutrient transfers in nutrition. `feedAssembly` uses the intestines' absorption-times-function rule.
- NOTE: function = minimum input adequacy times (1 - damage), following `damage_function.function-from-inputs` and respecting the ceiling in `two-numbers`. Inputs are sampled before publication so a graph cycle cannot depend on enumeration order. Call `anatomyProcess.step(world, 0)` after input-change events; daily damage/repair and collagen maintenance occur on accumulated day boundaries. Host insults are per-day increments, repair requires an explicit nutrition gate, and tissue integrity can be supplied by the host. Unconnected blood supply or unknown input returns null. A newly assembled, unchallenged body starts healthy; this is an initial condition, not proof of adequate environmental supply.
