## chemistry (2573)

- NOTE: atomic weights. `src/elements.ts` holds the IUPAC CIAAW standard atomic weights (https://ciaaw.org/atomic-weights.htm) as rounded representative values, not isotope masses. Where CIAAW gives an interval (H, C, N, O, S) the table uses the single conventional value; real samples vary inside that interval and the library does not model the variation.
- NOTE: element conservation is checked on atom moles (`World.elementMolesOf`: mass / molarMass x atoms per formula), so it holds for any molar mass a row carries; mass follows from the counts. A reaction moves `extent x coefficient` moles of each substance, the reaction's own integers. (2576 replaced the old split between NIST and rounded rows: see the weight-set note below.)
- NOTE: rows whose molar mass moved (water, h2, limestone, quicklime, slaked lime) keep their per-mole cp; the per-kg cp was rescaled to the new mass.
- FLAG: reaction oxygen condition. `comb_c_full` carries `minO2` 0.15 (oxygen volume fraction of the vessel's gas, below which it does not run), Rex's 2026-10-29 doc value; no opened source gives 15%, so `minO2Source` is null and the row's note marks it [model]. The rate by fuel surface and draught is the game's rule and stays in the game's adapter column. Per-kg heat is `heatPerKg` (heat over the first reactant's kg per mole), never stored: about 32.8 MJ/kg for charcoal at the row's -393510 J/mol and 0.012011 kg/mol.
- NOTE: one weight set (2576). Every substance row's `molarMass` is its formula summed with the CIAAW table in `src/elements.ts` (`formulaMolarMass`, rounded to 1e-9 kg/mol), and `physics.ts`'s `M` is built from the same function, so a balanced reaction also conserves total mass, not only atom moles (water formation lost about 1.8e-6 kg per mole of reaction while 2573 left o2, n2, co2, co, charcoal and ethanol rounded). The registry test holds every row to its formula at 1e-12, and the reaction tests assert total mass at 1e-12. Rows that moved: o2 0.031998, n2 0.028014, co2 0.044009, co 0.02801, charcoal 0.012011, ethanol 0.046069, h2s 0.034076, so2 0.064058, ch4 0.016043, s 0.25648 (S8), kno3 0.101102, h2 0.002016, limestone 0.100086, slaked lime 0.074092; each keeps its per-mole cp (per-kg cp rescaled). The NIST WebBook molecular weights in the rows' `checked` notes differ in the fifth or sixth digit (for example h2 2.01588, CO2 44.0095); the rows do not carry them, because two weight sets cannot both conserve mass. hhv stays per kg as cited.
- NOTE: (2579) M is one weight set rounded to 1e-9. `formulaMolarMass` was documented as rounded to 1e-9 but returned the raw float sum; it now rounds, so `M.H2O`, `M.O2`, `M.N2`, `M.CO2` and `M.CO` equal their registry rows exactly (asserted with `toBe` in `test/registry.test.ts`). `physiology.ts` (metabolic water, `* M.H2O`) and `atmosphere.ts` use `M` and nothing else for these substances, and the metabolic-water amount the stable's ledger records is the same number added to the body, so the weight was never what split them. The horse stable's 1.0e-9 kg water miss on day 27 was float drift: a constant ~1e-3 kg step added 2.3 million times onto a 285 kg water mass rounds the same way each time. `Registry.addContent` now keeps a Kahan carry per content entry (a WeakMap, so the contents' shape is unchanged); no tolerance changed.
- NOTE: (2577) `minO2` is a floor, not only a start condition. `comb_c_full` is a complete reaction, so one step at 21% oxygen used the limiting reagent and could burn the vessel's oxygen to 0%. `reactionProcess` now caps a step's extent to the one that lands exactly on `minO2` (solved from the vessel's gas moles and the row's gas oxygen and gas mole change per extent; no extent when already at or under it). Rows without `minO2` behave as before. Consequence: an oxygen-limited charcoal burn stops with 15% oxygen left in the vessel, and a vent that carries the CO2 away lets the fraction of what remains stay above the floor, so the burn continues at the rate the venting allows.
- NOTE: (2583) A vented vessel keeps burning past the minO2 floor because the vent replaces the gas; only a sealed vessel stops at 15%. Pinned by the vented 25% case in test/reactions.test.ts.
- NOTE: (2582) The Kahan carry in Registry.addContent (src/registry.ts) holds only while every contents change goes through addContent; a direct write to a contents entry's mass leaves a stale carry.
